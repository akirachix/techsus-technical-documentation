# Features

## Overview

**Redu** is a Progressive Web App for refugee learners in Uganda. MVP 1 covers **Mathematics, English, Biology, Physics and Chemistry**, and has five features that match the modules in the 
<a href="../assets/images/SAD.png" target="_blank">System Architecture Diagram</a>

| Feature | Used by | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Learn | Learner | Study curriculum lessons, with an AI tutor on hand |
| Assessments | Learner | Find learning gaps and get a catch-up plan |
| View progress | Learner | See how far you have come and what to do next |
| Offline packages | Learner | Download lessons and study without internet |
| System management | Administrator | Monitor usage, learners and curriculum coverage |

---

## Learn 

- **Subject and topic navigation:** Learners choose a subject and browse S1 and S2 topics in a simple, guided layout.
- **Curriculum-grounded lessons:** Lessons are built from approved curriculum files, with linked textbook images, so content stays on syllabus.
- **Flexible explanations:** Each concept can be shown as a simple explanation, step by step, a worked example, a visual or practice questions.
- **AI tutor:** Learners ask topic questions and get answers in simple English from approved content and reviewed glossary terms.
- **Recommended for you:** The home screen suggests the next topics based on the learner's results.

<a href="../assets/images/learn_feature.png" target="_blank">View learn screen </a>

---

## Assessments 

- **Diagnostic check:** A short assessment finds what a learner knows, struggles with and may have missed.
- **Practice quizzes:** Questions appear one at a time, with a progress bar so learners know how far along they are.
- **Instant evaluation:** Answers are evaluated straight away, with feedback and the correct answers.
- **Catch-up plan:** Feedback comes with a plan that points to the topics that need work.
- **Fair results:** Diagnostic results are tested across learner groups so no group is unfairly disadvantaged.

<a href="../assets/images/assessments_feature.png" target="_blank">View assessments screen </a>

---

## View Progress 

- **Progress summary:** Completed lessons, quiz results and topics mastered in one place.
- **Topics needing practice:** Weaker areas are highlighted so learners know where to focus.
- **Next steps:** Every summary ends with a recommended next activity.
- **Recent activity:** Learners can look back over what they studied recently.
- **Improvement over time:** Baseline and follow-up assessments show how much a learner has improved.

<a href="../assets/images/progress_feature.png" target="_blank">View progress screen </a>

---

## Offline Packages

- **Download by subject, class, topic and term:** Learners choose exactly what they need.
- **Study without internet:** Downloaded lessons are saved on the device and open without a connection.
- **Automatic sync:** Progress made offline is saved locally and sent to the server when the connection returns.
- **Works on shared devices:** The app runs on phones, tablets and school computers, and clears local data when a learner logs out.
- **Trusted packages:** Packages are signed, versioned and checked for tampering before use.

<a href="../assets/images/offline_feature.png" target="_blank">View offline screen </a>

---

## System Management (Admin PWA)

- **System overview:** Active learners, usage trends and system health at a glance.
- **Usage and engagement analytics:** System-wide data shows how learners use Redu.
- **Learner management:** A table of learners with their device and status.
- **Curriculum coverage:** Coverage by subject shows where content is complete.
- **Privacy-safe reporting:** Reports on small groups are suppressed so no learner can be identified.
